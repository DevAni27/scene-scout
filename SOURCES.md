# Research provenance

The prepared Pune shortlist was assembled on 27 October 2026 from actual opened sources. Plan sources contain URLs, access dates and narrow support statements. It deliberately does not claim confirmed filming permission, live crowd levels, exact entrances or on-site observations.

- Pune District government, Khadakwasala Dam: identifies the Mutha River dam and nearby Panshet/Varasgaon. https://pune.gov.in/en/tourist-place/khadakwasala-dam/
- Maharashtra Department of Tourism, Panshet Dam: describes general tourism setting and road approach; the nearby Varasgaon description supports the replacement's water/green-hill creative interpretation. It does not establish commercial crew access. https://maharashtratourism.gov.in/nature/panshet-dam/
- Maps of India, Khadakwasla Dam: published approximate coordinates, used as an area reference, not an entrance. https://www.mapsofindia.com/maps/dams/khadakwasla-dam.html
- Wikipedia Pashan Lake, Panshet Dam and Varasgaon Dam: openly read place/coordinate references; treat as approximate secondary references. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pashan_Lake ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Panshet_Dam ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Varasgaon_Dam

Conflicting Khadakwasla coordinates were found during research: its Wikipedia coordinate pointed far west of other references. That coordinate is NOT used. A government PDF search result corroborated the longitude vicinity, but full retrieval failed; the PDF is not presented as an opened source. The package uses the opened Maps of India coordinate and requires on-site position checks. No latitude/longitude is a verified public filming point.

The fictional creative request is a proposed shoot brief, not fabricated factual observations. The sites themselves are real. Creative fit/trade-offs are interpretations based on sourced context; facility and permission gaps remain unknown. Maps and camera framing are scouting context, not safety/access instructions.

No source-site images are bundled. Optional user-supplied or licensed photos must retain author/source/license information. No generated imagery depicts a claimed real location. Unit-test images are explicitly synthetic fixtures and are not candidate evidence.

KML camera, ScreenOverlay, IconStyle, NetworkLink and XML conventions use Google's primary KML reference: https://developers.google.com/kml/documentation/kmlreference . Close views use LookAt with altitudeMode relativeToGround; no street-level camera or physically accurate lighting is claimed.

The transport is adapted from the contributor's tested Globe Classroom transport, including empty-before-unlink cleanup. New SceneScout live deployment still requires contributor acceptance. Classroom's successful live run is not represented as a live SceneScout result.
